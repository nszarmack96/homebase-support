Privacy in HomeBase.

HomeBase is a home-maintenance app by Nicholas Szarmack. It has no separate HomeBase account or developer-operated record server.

Effective October 28, 2026.

Your home records

HomeBase stores homes, maintenance tasks, completion history, notes, costs, provider details, equipment information and imported documents in the app’s storage on your device.

This release does not sync records through iCloud. Exports leave the app when you choose a destination. You control where exported copies are saved.

Photos and documents

HomeBase imports the photos and documents you choose. Imported photos are processed to remove location metadata before saving; a photo is refused if that removal fails. Documents and receipts may still contain private information in their visible content.

Exports and reminders

Exports are prepared on your device and handed to the system share sheet. They leave the app when you choose a destination. HomeBase schedules reminders locally; default reminder text does not name the task.

Purchases

Apple processes HomeBase Plus subscriptions. HomeBase uses verified StoreKit transaction information to determine access. HomeBase does not receive your payment-card details. Subscription management remains with your Apple Account.

Analytics and tracking

HomeBase uses no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. It does not operate an analytics or crash-reporting service. Apple’s own services and any diagnostics you choose to share are governed by Apple’s settings and policies.

Your choices

You can export your records and delete a home from Settings → Homes. Deleting a home or the app does not cancel a subscription. Removing the app does not delete exports or copies saved to other destinations. Manage those separately where you saved them.

If you contact support, you choose what to send. Avoid passwords, payment information and full data exports; remove private details from screenshots.

Children

HomeBase is not directed to children.

Contact and changes

Privacy contact: nszarmack2+homebase@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be accompanied by an updated effective date.
