A little help with HomeBase.

Your maintenance plan, equipment details and care history, in one place.

Contact support

Email nszarmack2+homebase@gmail.com for HomeBase support.

When contacting support, describe the screen and what happened. Include your HomeBase version and iOS version if available. Do not send passwords, payment details or a full home-data export. Remove addresses, serial numbers and other private information from screenshots.

Where are my records?

This release stores your home records on your device and does not sync them through iCloud. Keep an export of important records.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings and choose Export a copy of everything, then Full data export. Save the ZIP to Files or another destination you choose. Unzip it in Files to see home.json, manifest.json and any exported attachments. This data archive is different from the HomeBase Plus PDF home-care report.

What happens when Plus ends?

Your existing homes and records remain readable, exportable and deletable. You can complete existing tasks and keep their reminders. Adding beyond the free plan’s counts is restricted, and advanced history filters turn off. Search remains free.

How do I cancel or restore Plus?

Manage cancellation through your iPhone’s Apple Account subscription settings. Canceling renewal normally leaves access until the current period ends. Deleting HomeBase or its records does not cancel a subscription. To check an existing purchase, use Restore purchases in HomeBase’s subscription settings.

How do I delete a home?

Open Settings, then Homes, select the home and choose Delete. Export anything you want to keep first. Archiving a home is different from deleting it.

About maintenance suggestions

Suggested intervals are starting points. Follow your equipment’s instructions and use a qualified professional when appropriate. HomeBase is a personal record, not a safety inspection or certification.
